# Almost Abandoning D4 Too Early

**What happened**: After 14 trades, D4 was showing net +$47 and I seriously considered abandoning it. Three losing trades in a row felt like the strategy was broken.

**Impact**: I almost threw away what turned out to be the best strategy found through months of research — because I couldn't handle 14 trades of variance.

**Fix**: Nothing technical. I had to learn patience the hard way. The next 5 trades were all winners.

**Lesson**: 27 trades is noise. 100 trades is a conversation. 500 trades is evidence. Drawing conclusions from single-digit sample sizes is a fast way to make bad decisions — in trading and in engineering.
